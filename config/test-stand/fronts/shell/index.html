shell — crm and cart in frames

The shell is a front of the broker itself (oidc-client-ts, client shell), and so are the fronts in the frames, on their own origins (:5183, :5184). The provider's login page does not show in a frame, so the login (and, when wanted, the logout) is done by the shell at the top level; the frames then open on the gateway's session: cart by userinfo, crm by prompt=none.
